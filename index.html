Questions

What age are the maths workbooks for?

They are made for Upper Kindergarten (UKG), about ages 4 to 6, with large print and simple instructions. Sit with your child for the first few pages.

How do I print the workbook?

Print on A4 paper at Actual size (100%). For the double-sided file, choose "flip on long edge". The blank backs stop pencil and crayon showing through.

Is this a physical book?

No. All our books are digital PDF files that you print at home or read on a screen. Nothing is shipped.

I paid but did not get my book. What should I do?

Please email or WhatsApp us with the email address or phone number you used and your payment confirmation. We will sort it out quickly.

Can I share the PDF with friends or other classes?

One purchase is for one family or one teacher. Please do not share or resell the files. If your school wants a multi-user licence, contact us.
